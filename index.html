How To Find A Local News Story (That Isn’t A Fluff Piece)

How to Find a Good Local News Story

Assembly time: 60 minutes
Difficulty rating: ★ ★ ★ ☆ ☆

It is so easy find and create fluff stories on a local level, especially as a student. However, If you want to get notived by large news networks, you should consider digging into hard stories. And yes, small towns can be crawling with hard stories, you just need to know where to look.

  • Things You'll Need

  • Notebook (for writting contact information)
  • Business Card Holder
  • Social Media Account
  • Recording Equipment

  1. Stay Informed

    1. Stay Informed When looking for a newsworthy story, it’s best to stay informed about the world at large. There will be times where a nationwide story will impact the local level. For example, in 2025 during the longest government shut-down at the time, SNAP benefits were not distributed for the month of November. Leaving millions of Americans without food. There are several food pantries that are more than willing to do interviews about this topic due to the severe impact. Politics isn’t the only thing that affects small towns. So keep an eye on what’s going on in the world.

  2. Take a Walk Around Your Community!

    When you walk into locally owned businesses, they often have posters for events or business cards for other community members. The same goes for college campuses’ although you are more likely to find fluffier pieces this way. However, people are always talking. Sometimes you can find a story by listening in. There may be stories that come from observing the environment. Takes notes and check on it later

  3. Check Social Media Pages!

    Be sure to have a profile on every social media site. We’re moving to a more digitally connected era where every town has their own page. Follow the official accounts that are run by the city and police, but also look out for community run pages. Sites like Reddit may have a sub page for your town or even college. There may also be accounts that are dedicated to sharing public comments.

  4. Record EVERYTHING

    Cameras are extremely common today. Whether it’s for security or tiktok, someone will always be recording. And sometimes they catch something that you miss. It could be as simple as a nat pop (sound that grabs someone’s attention) or a quote that completes a story. There are also moments that can’t be repeated. One unexpected shot could lead you down the rabbit hole of your next beat.

  5. Have you Checked With Past Sources?

    Believe it or not, people you’ve talked to in the past would love to talk again. Life is constantly in motion, meaning things change. If you haven’t checked in with old sources, now might be the time! You never know when a new story will come out of it. Plus, the more you talk with them, the stronger your relationship will be which could lead to your source coming to you.
